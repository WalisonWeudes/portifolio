# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, served from the portfolio's `public/exemplos/medica-estetica/` folder (Vite). No framework at runtime. Same setup as the other examples.

## Users

Independent professionals (doctors, aesthetic clinics, therapists) in Piauí who reach this page from Walison Weudes's portfolio by clicking "Ver exemplo" on the "Profissional liberal" product card. They judge what a personal-brand site would look like for them.

Inside the demo, the implied visitor is a woman (mostly 25–55) in Teresina considering an aesthetic procedure, who wants to trust the doctor before booking.

## Product Purpose

A realistic one-page site for a fictional aesthetic doctor, "Dra. Helena Duarte · Medicina Estética", Teresina — PI. Job: build trust (credentials, approach, safety) and turn the visit into a WhatsApp booking. Card promises: Especialidades, Agendamento, Localização.

## Positioning

A proof of craft: editorial, fashion-magazine feel instead of the generic pink spa template. Interactive hook: a face map (blurred portrait with sharp windows); clicking an area (eyes, lips, skin, contour) shows the indicated procedures.

## Operating Context

- Opened in a new tab from the portfolio; a top bar links back to the portfolio's Products section.
- One page. Booking builds a pre-filled WhatsApp message; since the doctor is fictional, the demo shows a preview instead of opening a real number.
- Most visitors browse on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fictional doctor; labeled as an example; no real CRM number (show a clearly fake placeholder).
- Photos from Unsplash (free license) via CDN, credited in the footer.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only.
- No promises of results; medical-ad tone (CFM): no "antes e depois" sensationalism, no prices of procedures.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette (binding, owner's second round): sky blue from the "DAYDREAM" reference (#A9C3D5) replaces the wine red; Light Sand #F5EBD0 stays for the panels; deep blue #2C4A66 for text accents and buttons.
- Hero (binding): "DAYDREAM"-style: giant thin serif name behind the portrait, mono corner labels with crosshair lines, coordinates, rotating round badge, floating figure like the reference's bird.
- Page style (binding): presentation-deck style: sand panels with rounded corners on the blue page, big photos with cards over them, headings mixing sans with serif italic.
- Kept on request: tilted photo fan (consultório), the "Sobre" section with portrait and italic quote, the face map with the orbit and blurred lens.
- Classical layer (owner's third round, references: Cassandra/Anchika/Regalia/Rosery): high-resolution classical paintings and marble sculpture from Unsplash in arch, pill and oval frames with thin double lines, four-point sparkles, the name crossing a pill in the footer. The owner's first figures were too low-resolution and were dropped. Only the angel statue is a cut-out (`img/anjo-estatua.webp`, keyed from its own blue sky).
- Type (binding): Rokurou Display (Pixelbuddha, free for websites, credited in the footer) is the main display face, set in capitals because its lowercase "c" reads like "e"; Cormorant Garamond italic for accents and quotes; Hanken Grotesk for body text; IBM Plex Mono for small labels.

## Evidence on Hand

All testimonials, numbers and credentials are fictional placeholders for the demo.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before ask: credentials and approach precede every booking call.
2. Naturalness: copy speaks of natural results, safety and evaluation, never transformation hype.
3. Honest demo: always identifiable as an example built by Walison Weudes.
