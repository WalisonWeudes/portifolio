# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, served from the portfolio's `public/` folder (Vite). No framework at runtime. Same setup as the "Site institucional" example in `exemplos/site-institucional/`.

## Users

Owners of small local businesses (Picos and the rest of Piauí, Brazil) who reach this page from Walison Weudes's portfolio by clicking "Ver exemplo" on the "Landing page para empresa" product card. They are deciding whether to hire Walison for a one-page landing for their own company, and use this demo to judge what they would get.

Inside the demo, the implied visitor is a homeowner, shop owner or farmer in Piauí with a high electricity bill, considering solar energy. (Inferred: the owner did not answer the business-type question; solar was the proposed default.)

## Product Purpose

A realistic, finished-looking one-page landing for a fictional solar-energy installer, "Raia Energia Solar" ("Raia"). Its single job is conversion: turn a visit into a WhatsApp conversation for a free quote. Success: a small-business owner leaves thinking "a single page can sell this well", and goes back to the portfolio to get in touch.

## Positioning

A proof of craft, not a template: one page, one goal, with an interactive hook (a savings simulator from the visitor's own electricity bill) that a static template would not have.

## Operating Context

- Opened in a new tab from the portfolio; a top bar links back to the portfolio's Products section.
- One page only. Every call to action leads to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message (inferred default). Since the company is fictional, the demo shows a preview of the message instead of opening a real number.
- Most visitors browse on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fictional company; content must be labeled as an example and must not imitate a real firm.
- Photos come from Unsplash (free license), loaded from the Unsplash CDN, with credit in the footer.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only.
- Savings figures are estimates from simple published-style assumptions and must be labeled as estimates.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner direction (binding): serious, because it is a company, but interactive and interesting.
- Palette (binding, supplied by the owner): #F74A28 (orange-red), #F7F8FF (off-white), #D6FA52 (lime).
- Interactive components inspired by React Bits (reactbits.dev), as requested by the owner.
- Reference bar supplied by the owner: Spaciaz (real estate), Creatix (creative agency), BizSolutionz (web agency).

## Evidence on Hand

All testimonials, client names, numbers and team members are fictional placeholders for the demo. Do not present them as real anywhere outside this example.

## Product Principles

1. One goal: every section moves the visitor toward the WhatsApp conversation.
2. Show the money: the visitor's own savings, not abstract benefits.
3. Trust before ask: proof (installations, guarantees, process) sits next to every call to action.
4. Honest demo: always identifiable as an example built by Walison Weudes.
