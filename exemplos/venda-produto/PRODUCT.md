# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, served from the portfolio's `public/` folder (Vite). No framework at runtime. Same setup as the other examples in `exemplos/`.

## Users

Owners of small businesses that sell products (Picos, Piauí and beyond) who reach this page from Walison Weudes's portfolio by clicking "Ver exemplo" on the "Venda de produto" card. They are judging what a sales page built by Walison would look like for their own product.

Inside the demo, the implied visitor is a coffee drinker in Brazil buying specialty coffee online for home: beans or ground coffee, and brewing accessories.

## Product Purpose

A realistic, finished-looking product sales page for a fictional specialty-coffee roaster, "Cerejal Cafés Especiais". Its job is conversion: choose a coffee, choose the grind, add to cart. Success: a business owner leaves thinking "my product could sell like this", and goes back to the portfolio to get in touch.

## Positioning

A proof of craft: a store page that helps the buyer decide (roast, grind, brewing method) instead of only listing products.

## Operating Context

- Opened in a new tab from the portfolio; a top bar links back to the portfolio's Products section.
- One page. The cart works inside the page; checkout is a demo and charges nothing.
- Most visitors browse on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fictional company; content must be labeled as an example and must not imitate a real brand.
- Photos come from Unsplash (free license), loaded from the Unsplash CDN, with credit in the footer.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only. Prices in reais.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette (binding, supplied by the owner): #104F02 (coffee-leaf green), #FFF4F0 (cream), #FF7B3D (orange).
- Product line (binding): coffee for sale — ground coffee, beans and related items.
- Reference bar supplied by the owner: PHLOX (colored category tiles, product hero with giant word behind, sale banner), Voltix (category row, featured collections, trending grid with add to cart, benefits row), Lixma (product hero with floating price card, product lineup cards with specs, dark feature band, community block).
- Standing preferences from earlier examples: interactive and interesting, but serious; avoid generic sections that look like the other examples; no testimonial carousel.

## Evidence on Hand

All products, prices, scores, reviews and numbers are fictional placeholders for the demo. Do not present them as real anywhere outside this example.

## Product Principles

1. Help the buyer choose: roast, grind and brewing method explained by doing, not by text.
2. Cart always one tap away, with honest totals and the free-shipping threshold visible.
3. Origin and freshness as proof: roast date, farm, score.
4. Honest demo: always identifiable as an example built by Walison Weudes.
