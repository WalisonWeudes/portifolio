Scope: home page (index.html) of the Ventura Contabilidade institutional example; inner pages (servicos, sobre, contato) inherit the world. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: small-business owner in Piauí, often on a phone, judging whether this firm (and the site's maker) is serious and modern. Primary action: request a conversation with an accountant. Proof: services with real photos, figures, process, deadlines tracked, client testimonials, example plans (all fictional, labeled).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a modern service-business site, played straight at the craft level of the owner's references (Logamax, Lafarge, PureShine, SalesSync, Growify). It refuses the generic template's weak spots: stock-blue palette, empty icon tiles with no imagery, and a testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: White and soft mint-grey grounds; deep forest green #0a2e22 for dark bands and footer; brand green #0f7a4a for primary actions and highlighted words; a lime accent #c6ec7a used only on dark green for small highlights. Manrope 500 to 800, tight display tracking. Rounded 16 to 24px cards with soft offset shadows; pill buttons; real Unsplash photography in every service card and the hero.

STORY: The owner sees a trustworthy, established firm, understands the services from photo cards, sees the numbers and the four-step process, notices the deadlines are tracked, reads three client testimonials and the example plans, then requests a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim utility bar (phone, e-mail, hours). White nav with logo, links and a green "Falar com um contador" pill. Hero: left, a large headline with two words in brand green, one support line, a primary and a secondary button, and an avatar row with the rating; right, a tall rounded photo of an accountant with two floating cards (companies served, average tax reduction) and a round call button. Three info cards overlap the hero's bottom edge.

FORM: The category standard (the canon card), chosen by the owner after the Guia Fiscal build; seed:4fde1ba2f33d3295. Signature interactions: a logo loop of client brands, figures counting up in the dark stats band, and the live monthly deadlines panel kept from the previous round.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
