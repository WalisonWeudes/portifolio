# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS pages generated by a small Node script, served from the portfolio's `public/` folder (Vite). No framework at runtime.

## Users

Owners of small local businesses (Picos and the rest of Piauí, Brazil) who reach this page from Walison Weudes's portfolio by clicking "Ver exemplo" on the "Site institucional" product card. They are deciding whether to hire Walison to build a site for their own company, and use this demo to judge what they would get.

Inside the demo, the implied visitor is a business owner looking for an accounting firm.

## Product Purpose

A realistic, finished-looking institutional website for a fictional accounting firm, "Ventura Contabilidade". Success: a small-business owner leaves thinking "I want my company's site to look this professional and trustworthy", and goes back to the portfolio to get in touch.

## Positioning

A proof of craft, not a template: a site that reads as a real, established, serious local firm rather than a generic or AI-made page.

## Operating Context

- Opened in a new tab from the portfolio; a top bar links back to the portfolio's Products section.
- Pages: Início (home), Serviços, Sobre, Contato. Forms are demos and send nothing.
- Visitors will compare it with sites they already know from banks, clinics, law and accounting firms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fictional company; content must be labeled as an example and must not imitate a real firm.
- Photos come from Unsplash (free license), loaded from the Unsplash CDN, with credit in the footer.
- Must work on phones, since most local business owners browse on mobile.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ventura Contabilidade. Location used in copy: Av. Central, 1200, Picos — PI (fictional).
- Owner direction (binding): serious and corporate, but with personality; colors that convey seriousness; no testimonial carousel.
- Standing preference (binding, chosen after the "Guia Fiscal" round felt too impersonal): the category standard for modern service-business landing pages, executed at full craft. Reference bar supplied by the owner: Logamax (3PL/warehouse), Lafarge Roofing, RoofPro, PureShine Cleaning, SalesSync (CRM), Growify (marketing agency). Traits to match: big real photo in the hero with floating info cards, one strong brand colour, photo service cards, dark stats band, process steps, testimonial cards, pricing, two-column FAQ, colour CTA band, dark footer with newsletter.

## Evidence on Hand

All testimonials, client names, numbers (320 empresas, 17 anos, -23% em impostos) and team members are fictional placeholders for the demo. Do not present them as real anywhere outside this example.

## Product Principles

1. Trust first: every element should make the firm look established, organized and reliable.
2. Clarity over decoration: a busy owner should understand services and how to start in seconds.
3. One clear next step: talking to an accountant.
4. Honest demo: always identifiable as an example built by Walison Weudes.
